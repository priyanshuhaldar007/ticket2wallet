Ticket2Wallet

Turn an IRCTC e-ticket PDF into an Apple Wallet pass. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

1. Your signing certificate not loaded

Apple only accepts passes signed with a Pass Type ID certificate from a (paid) Apple Developer account. One time setup, see the README. Files stay on your device.

2. Upload ticket

PDF from IRCTC (or a screenshot of it). We read the details and the QR code from it.
